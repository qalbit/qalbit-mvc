# Blog Content Clusters – CRM / ERP / MVP

Publish-ready WordPress drafts supporting the three new service pages. Each file has a
metadata block (title, slug, meta title/description, target keywords, internal links)
followed by the full article in Markdown.

## Why these exist

The CRM, ERP and MVP service pages target "company"-intent keywords. Pages without
topical support rarely crack page 1 for those terms. Each cluster article:

1. Targets an easier, high-intent long-tail query (cost / comparison / timeline)
2. Links UP to its service page with exact-match anchor text
3. Links to the cost calculator (`/tools/software-development-cost-calculator/`)

## Interlinking rules

- Every article links to its service page in the first 300 words AND in the conclusion.
- Every article links to the cost calculator at least once.
- Cost articles link to their comparison sibling and vice versa.
- After publishing, add a link FROM the service page FAQ/overview to the cost article
  ("See our detailed CRM cost guide") – ask the dev team or QalbIT's Claude session.

## After publishing each article

1. Request indexing in Search Console.
2. Add the article to the relevant category (create "CRM", "ERP", "Startup MVP" categories).
3. Set the meta title/description from the metadata block (Rank Math / Yoast).
